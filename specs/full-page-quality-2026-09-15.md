# Full-page quality repair plan - 2026-09-15

## Product contract

Every registered route must:

1. render without an uncaught exception at desktop and 390px widths;
2. expose one obvious primary task or a clear read-only boundary;
3. distinguish loading, empty, unavailable, stale, and success states;
4. preserve provider observation time and source provenance;
5. keep independent sections usable when a sibling request fails;
6. reject stale asynchronous responses after route/filter/selection changes;
7. provide a local retry or a precise recovery destination;
8. never turn historical research into trade intent or real-order behavior.
